HOW I WORK

Get close to the problem.
Then work out what needs to change.

I spend time with the people doing the work, understand how things behave today and help the team decide what needs to change. We test our understanding and adjust as we learn.

UNDERSTAND THE REAL PROCESS

Start with what actually happens.

I talk to users, walk through their work and look for the friction. We compare what happens in practice with what the process says should happen.

WORK THROUGH THE COMPLEXITY

Get into the process, system and data detail.

I connect the process, business rules, data and system behaviour. We work through gaps, dependencies and edge cases together, making the complexity visible enough to actually discuss.

BRIDGE BUSINESS AND DELIVERY

Turn the need into something the team can build.

I work with business and delivery teams to shape clear requirements, stories and acceptance criteria. Diagrams are often my favourite way to get everyone looking at the same problem.

STAY WITH IT THROUGH DELIVERY

Keep checking that it works in the real world.

I refine the backlog, work through questions and stay involved through testing and UAT. What we learn from users feeds back into what the team does next.

The work rarely moves neatly from discovery to delivery. I expect to go back, test assumptions and adjust as we learn more.
